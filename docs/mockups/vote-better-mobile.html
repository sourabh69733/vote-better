Why keep work separate from a person's profile?

Departments deliver many services. Representatives may ask, fund or advocate. The product should record each role without giving one person credit for another office's work.

One issue, clear stages

Example streetlight issue

Resident report → official channel identified → resident authorizes submission → official reference received → response recorded → outcome checked.

Private: resident identity, contact and exact home location. Public only with consent: approximate area and documented status.

What if no official joins the group?

The group still helps residents find the correct official channel. It must not pretend that a message was delivered or acknowledged. Automated forwarding would need verified addresses, consent, rate limits and proof of delivery.

Resident path

Suggest a correction

Point to the exact fact, explain the concern, and attach a source. The published fact stays visible with a review state and correction history.

Submitted → Reviewing → Decision → Public correction log
Public figure path

Claim a profile

Verify identity and role first. Then publish priorities, responses and links in a clearly marked “From this person” section. Their statements do not replace official records.

Reader path

Follow updates

Choose an area or person to follow. Notifications would distinguish a new official record, a person-authored post and a correction.

What if a public figure disputes a record?

Show the dispute and response alongside the original source. An editor checks the evidence and records the decision. Neither an accusation nor a denial becomes a verified fact by being posted.

Trust and data flow

Show how each fact was made

The design only works if a reader can inspect the original record and understand what it proves.

1Original record

Election result, affidavit, parliamentary record or documented government update.

2Person and area match

Check that the record belongs to the same person, office, term and constituency.

3Review the claim

Record the exact claim, source URL, document date, review date and what cannot be inferred.

4Publish with history

Show corrections and leave uncertain or unavailable fields empty.

Design rules

Facts vs statements

Person-authored updates get a clear label. They are not presented as independently verified outcomes.

Missing stays missing

No zero, green badge or score is inferred from unavailable data.
