Vote Better, starting in Jaipur

Help Indian voters understand who is asking for their vote through verified, comparable facts. Start with one constituency and one election type; grow only after the evidence and trust process work.

Research status: This document identifies usable sources and proposed product rules. It is not a published voter guide or a verified candidate ranking. The current repository contains demonstration candidate data and placeholder source links; none of it should appear as real Jaipur evidence.

A practical first slice

Recommended

Jaipur Lok Sabha constituency

Use the 2024 election as a historical evidence test. Rajasthan publishes a Jaipur return of election and candidate expenditure records. ECI provides candidate affidavits; PRS tracks the elected MP. Before a future vote, refresh the final contesting list and every profile.

Jaipur and Jaipur Rural are different parliamentary constituencies. We must never merge them.

Product boundary

Compare the actual choice

Show every final contesting candidate, not only major parties. Make the source, date, and missing fields visible. Past parliamentary activity applies only to people who previously served; an absent record is not a zero.

Two kinds of information

Verified record: election filing, official result, parliamentary record, or documented public record. Candidate voice: a claimed profile, priorities, responses, and work portfolio, visibly attributed to its author. Both can be useful; only the former is presented as independently verified.

Meaningful outcome

Test whether voters can identify the candidates, explain the basis for a comparison, spot missing evidence, and distinguish a filed fact from a candidate claim. Never infer a “good person” solely from a numerical score.

Proposed order of information

This is an editorial proposal for what voters see first, not a scientific ranking of what every voter should value. Each category can expand into detailed fields.

1

Identity and ballot status

Final candidate name, party or independent status, election, constituency, and nomination status. Establish that the person is actually on the ballot.

Essential
2

Criminal disclosures with legal status

Self-declared cases, sections, court, stage, and conviction where documented. Distinguish allegation, charge, pending case, closed case, and conviction.

Essential
3

Record in elected office, if applicable

Attendance, questions, debates, bills, role and term, with a comparable time window and context. Do not penalize first-time candidates for lacking an incumbent record.

Conditional
4

Affidavit finances and interests

Declared assets, liabilities, income and previous filings where comparable. Show self-declared status, dates, and household scope. Growth alone does not establish wrongdoing.

Essential
5

Public work and local outcomes

Documented recommendations, approved projects, completion and spending, with the institution responsible for each step. Separate an MP’s recommendation from district execution.

Attribution
6

Experience, education, past elections

Filed qualifications, prior roles, election history and vote totals. Education is descriptive; a degree is not proof of governing ability.

Context
7

Positions, promises and responses

Direct candidate statements and dated commitments, linked to the original statement and labelled as claims. Provide equal publishing access and a visible correction history.

Claim
8

Campaign finance and public documents

Filed expenditure, party disclosures and other records when available and meaningfully comparable. Show limitations before calculating derived measures.

Later

Sources checked for the Jaipur pilot

Rajasthan CEO · Jaipur 2024 return of election (Form 21E)

Official result document for parliamentary constituency 7, Jaipur. Baseline for who contested and votes received. Verify names against the final candidate list before publication.

ECI · Candidate Affidavit Portal

ECI says the portal exposes nomination status, candidate profiles and submitted affidavits. First source for self-declared assets, liabilities, education and criminal disclosures. A filing is a candidate declaration, not an independent finding of truth.

Rajasthan CEO · candidate expenditure

Lists Jaipur 2024 candidates and links to election expenditure statements. Useful for a later finance section after checking completeness and definitions.

ADR MyNeta · Jaipur comparison

Parsed affidavit data makes comparisons easier. Use as a cross-check against the original ECI filing; MyNeta itself warns that its archive may differ from current status. The viewed page does not list every name shown in Rajasthan’s official 2024 expenditure list, so it cannot define candidate coverage by itself.

PRS · Jaipur MP activity

Attendance, debates, questions and bills for the current MP, with period and national/state comparisons. PRS explains its counting rules in its methodology note.

MPLADS · dashboard

Tracks works and expenditure. Its own note says MPs recommend works while district authorities sanction, execute and complete them. We should not attribute expenditure directly to an MP.

Verification rule: For every displayed field store the exact source URL or document, page or record locator, filing period, retrieval time, transformation rule, reviewer, and correction history. If verification fails or a source disappears, hide the value and show “Data unavailable.”

Explore a transparent score policy

Why one “good candidate” number is hard

Facts can be measured; their importance and interpretation involve judgment. A criminal allegation is not a conviction. Attendance does not measure quality. New candidates lack parliamentary records. A universal score would hide these differences.

Recommendation: launch a fact comparison first. Add an optional, explainable score only for verified, comparable measures, and show coverage beside it.

Illustrative weights

Move sliders to see how the weighting choice changes. These are design choices, not candidate scores or research findings.

Proposed score gate: No score when the required source or denominator is missing; no zero for missing data. Score only within the same election and role. Show every underlying measure, weight, date and uncertainty. Keep declared cases as a detailed fact until a fair method for legal status is agreed. Candidate-written content never changes the verified score.

From voting information to ongoing accountability

A proposed second product area for 2027–2030. The first pilot should test routing and tracking with one problem category before promising action by every office.

Existing system

Route into the authority’s channel

Rajasthan Sampark 181 already accepts and tracks grievances. Rajasthan’s own documentation describes allocation, feedback, reopening and automatic escalation. CPGRAMS covers many central and state public authorities. We should guide residents to the correct official path and retain a reference number with consent. Direct submission or status syncing requires an authorized integration; neither is assumed.

Concrete example

Maharashtra FDA

The FDA links to its complaint portal, which offers registration and tracking. Its public interface describes voice or text complaint drafting in Marathi, Hindi and English, then routing to an officer. Public reports describe escalation on delay; we have not independently verified its internal escalation rules.

Different roles, different accountability

Elected representative: raise, recommend, scrutinize and explain action within their office’s powers. Department or officer: accept, assign, investigate and deliver a service within statutory authority. Show who owns the next action. Never mark a politician as the operator of a department simply because a resident tagged them.

Public work register

Each project can show a proposal, authority, budget document, sanction, contractor or delivery office where published, milestones, photos or inspection records, and resident feedback. Each update has an actor, timestamp and source. “Announced,” “sanctioned,” “work started,” and “completed” are separate states.

Illustrative issue lifecycle

Select a stage to see what evidence would justify displaying it. This is a design model, not a live Jaipur case.

Public by default only for safe facts

Public: issue category, approximate area, responsible office, official reference and dated status when the resident permits it. Private: complainant identity, exact home location, contact details, sensitive evidence and private correspondence. Remove personal details from shared cards.

What proves progress?

Measure correct routing, official acknowledgments, time to first response, cases reopened, independently verified resolutions, and resident satisfaction. A closed ticket or a video view count is not proof that the problem was solved.

3–4 year direction: Start with voter evidence → add candidate-authored profiles with verification → pilot issue routing and public work records in Jaipur → seek formal data-sharing or workflow partnerships → expand across offices and elections. This sequence is a proposal, not a forecast that integration will be available.

Pilot research and decisions

Establish a clean Jaipur dataset

Use final 2024 candidate records and affidavits. Match identities across ECI, Rajasthan CEO and ADR; log mismatches. Remove the repository’s sample profiles from any public route.

Define verification and correction rules

Two-person review for sensitive claims, provenance per field, versioned corrections, appeals and a visible response timeline. Candidate accounts must verify identity and clearly label self-published claims.

Test the comparison with voters

Mobile Hindi and English prototype, all candidates equally represented, concise summary with source expansion, share card linked to the live record. Check comprehension and whether people recognize missing or contested data.

Evaluate scoring before enabling it

Audit metric definitions, incentives and bias. Test whether the score changes understanding or merely creates false certainty. Publish code and methodology for review.

Expand by election and channel

Only after the pilot, add other constituencies and election types. Keep the same evidence schema; adapt role-specific performance measures. Consider an app after the website proves useful.
